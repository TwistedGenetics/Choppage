CHOPPAGE now lists breaks from the shared folder beside your plugins:
    VST2 Plugins/Content/Breaks
when you have made it yourself (CHOPPAGE never creates folders in VST2 Plugins). Put your WAV, AIFF or REX (.rx2 / .rex) breaks there.
Without that folder, or while it has no breaks, this folder is listed.
A tempo in the file name is read: amen_170.wav = 170 BPM.
